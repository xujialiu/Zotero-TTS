## An expanded layout transition reports the old bar height (2026-09-18 00:22, issue #124)

Research on installed 1.12.12-beta8 found the owner's paused EPUB player
in layout B with a 300 × 34 iframe around 300 × 202 content. The title
row remained visible; the transport started below the frame at y=41.
The complete evidence and source references are on [#124](https://github.com/xujialiu/Zotero-TTS/issues/124).

An isolated iframe loaded the installed resources and reproduced expanded
A → B three times, without touching the reading session. The child changed
its variant before `render()` closed the old popover. That close reported
the old layout-A DOM's 34px height before the new DOM replaced it.
`updateControls()` only reports height when the collapsed class changes;
the expanded panel never takes that branch. Waiting or sending an unchanged
snapshot did not correct it. The collapsed comparison did take the branch
and reported 108 after the stale 34. Notifying against the completed
floating DOM correctly measured 202.

This differs from the stale resource cache incident of September 16:
the installed resource has the expected controls and dimensions, but the
transition reports them in the wrong order. A layout identifier matching
the saved preference does not prove that its content fits the frame. The
position shortcut's previous live checks covered state preservation and
layout identifiers, leaving this expanded geometry condition uncovered.

Research probes are in `.tmp/zotero-dev/floating-height/`; they call the
real child renderer, not the keyboard listener. The keyboard trigger is
the owner's report and its route is confirmed in source. No production
fix was made. The recommendation is to report the completed layout's size
after rendering and avoid reporting the outgoing DOM during replacement;
implementation awaits the owner's decision.

Temporary frames were removed; no preferences, playback state, providers,
or installation were changed. Geometry required the already visible host;
it was minimized after research (`windowState=2`). The latest five console
entries predated the probes and contained no plugin error.

## Layout rendering reports its completed dimensions (2026-09-18 00:25, issue #124)

The approved fix closes an outgoing popover without reporting its old
dimensions, then reports height after `render()` installs the new controls
and applies the current expanded state. It uses the existing synchronous
callback, with no timer or new setting. The pre-fix Gecko probe is the red
regression baseline; the live case now explicitly checks actual frame and
content rectangles in addition to the shortcut's layout/state checks.

All 2,512 tests and typecheck passed. The candidate is 1.12.12-beta9;
live verification is pending. The manifest already had an uncommitted beta8
version from the preceding icon work; this candidate advances it to beta9.
