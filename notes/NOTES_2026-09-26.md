## Zotero's H and U annotate through two reader calls, and the segment must be the manager's own (2026-09-26 01:55, issue #145)

A reporter found H and U no longer annotating during playback with the
plugin on. The plugin now has keys of its own for it, Shift+H and
Shift+U, bindable like the rest. What they rest on, from Zotero
10.0.3-beta.3's `resource/reader/reader.js`:

- The bare keys live in the view's keydown (82110-82116), under
  `readAloudActive && !event.target.matches('button, select')`: a focus on
  any button — the plugin's Player included — swallows them before they
  are looked at. The annotate button of Zotero's hidden popup made the
  same call (38623-38628).
- The call is two steps: `_readAloudManager.getSegmentToAnnotate()` —
  the previous segment while under half and under 3 s into the current
  one (39386-39395; the Engine answers it from `session.ts`) — then
  `reader.addAnnotationFromReadAloudSegment(segment, type)` (84337). That
  one finds the segment by `this._readAloudManager.segments.indexOf`, so
  it must be the manager's own object: the Engine's controller holds the
  reader's segments waived (`controller.ts`), which unwrap to the same
  objects on the way back.
- The annotation is created at once (`_addAnnotationFromReadAloudSegments`),
  not on the popup's confirm; the popup is `readAloudState.annotationPopup`.
  A second call while it is open only retypes it
  (`setReadAloudAnnotationType`, 84553). A segment that already carries an
  annotation (`segmentAnnotations`) reopens that one's popup instead of
  adding another.
