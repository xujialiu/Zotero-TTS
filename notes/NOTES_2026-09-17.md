## Player following passed after waiting for the selected iframe (2026-09-17 00:11, issue #117)

The 1.12.12-beta candidate passed 2,445 automated tests, typecheck, build
and documentation rendering. The final XPI hash is
`8c0d43b3b634f23a8b29549a908f696ca11320f71c417e68ae4092735f7bbd9b`;
the embedded/on-disk bundle hash is
`82a928f7bb0ecca6cb163dad4e84b2babbfe108c319d48eadc219548cc7dc29a`.
The build test regenerated the artifact after the first recorded hashes;
the tester rejected that stale brief before installing and then used this
final pair. The owner requested the commit after live verification.

Live checks covered PDF and scrolled/paginated EPUB, sentence/outside styles,
A/top/B layouts, manual protection/reentry, paused stillness, manual-mode
persistence, document isolation, the retired global false preference, and
15 trusted paused return/sentence/paragraph shortcuts. The player snapshot,
visible letter, tooltip and ARIA state agreed. Fixture cleanup restored named
preference values/user flags and observed owner state, with no new relevant
plugin/dead-object errors.

Two reused fixed-delay PDF/outside rows initially looked like failures.
The resume trace had `pending:true`, the follower's hidden-view state.
A targeted rerun recorded the selected tab, host window state, view suspension
and iframe visibility. The iframe was initially hidden and became visible
on its third readiness sample. Once selected, nonminimized and visible, both
keep-following values resumed the first sentence to scrollTop 0 with a visible
fragment; later-visible reentry recovered within the measured 300 ms. No
production change was needed. Require real iframe readiness, not a fixed
sleep or nonempty geometry, before judging return/follow behavior.

The reusable kit is `test/zotero-dev/scripts/player-following/`; the obsolete
global-preference check in the plugin-player kit now points there. The
boundary diagnostic and assertion preserve the invalid and valid evidence.
Fixture AudioContexts remained suspended at currentTime 0: natural audio
progression and perceived scroll smoothness were not verified. Paginated
partial-spread rows lacking usable geometry remain not testable.
