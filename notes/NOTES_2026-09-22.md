## Brackets go wherever they enclose text; a `<…>` pair of two math signs stays (2026-09-22 00:43, issue #127)

Fish takes a square-bracketed group inside the text as an instruction: the
reply's `content` loses the words, the word timings skip them and the audio
is shorter (the issue's measurements, `s2.1-pro-free`). The rule of #94,
#96 and #101 removed brackets only when nothing but punctuation, symbols or
whitespace stood outside the groups, so a sentence with one word outside
reached Fish as written. That rule existed to protect comparisons.

`prepareSpeechText` now keeps the unpaired opening positions of each
configured pair; a closing symbol takes the nearest unpaired opening one of
its own pair, whatever lies between. Nested groups lose every layer, since
Fish would swallow an inner pair, and crossing groups (`<[Hello>]`) lose
both pairs. A symbol that closes several pairs closes the one opened last.
Unpaired symbols stay. The removed positions are sorted before they are
returned: `restoreSpeechOffsets` counts them in ascending order, and a
crossing group records them out of order. An opening symbol configured for
two pairs still leaves a segment that contains it unchanged, and an invalid
list still changes nothing.

The `<>` pair alone gets a math-sign test, the owner's choice over literal
removal, which turned `x <= 5 and y >= 3` into `x = 5 and y = 3` and
`aged < 65 years and BMI > 30` into two bare numbers. A `<` or `>` reads as
a sign when whitespace stands on both sides, an ASCII letter or digit on
both sides, `=` touches it, or `-` stands before a `>`. A sign-like `>`
pairs only with a sign-like `<`, and that pair stays as written; a plain
`>` takes the nearest plain `<` and falls back to the nearest sign-like one.
The fallback is for the owner's LitRPG stats, whose segments read
`< 100 exp>` and `< 2/50 HP >` (#98): their `<` is spaced, and without it
`You gained < 100 exp> today.` would keep its signs. Letters and digits are
ASCII on purpose — Chinese has no spaces, and `他获得了<火球术>技能` must
still lose its pair. #94's single wrapper `<a < b>` now falls out of the
rule without its special case, as does `<Warning: HP < 10%>`. Accepted
misreads: `You have < 2/50 HP > left.` stays (both signs spaced),
`List<String>` loses its pair, `P<.001 and Q>.05` loses both signs.

The cache is keyed by the prepared text, so a sentence whose preparation
changed is synthesized once more, and the entry under its old text is
never hit again. On the old scan 31 new or rewritten expectations failed.
The bracket fixture gained four paragraphs — the issue's two sentences, a
spaced angle label and a comparison — for case 3g item 10. Live results
are recorded separately.

## Zotero asks for its voice list only signed in, and every plugin voice rode that request (2026-09-22 02:46, issue #130)

Reported from outside: signed out of Zotero, no plugin voice reached the
player — not System's, not a local Fish Speech server's — while Test
connection passed, the voice browser listed the voices and nothing was
logged. Read in the installed 10.0.3 (rj = `resource/reader/reader.js`,
xr = `chrome/content/zotero/xpcom/reader.js`):

- `ReadAloudManager.loadVoices(loadRemote)` (rj:82310-82322) calls the
  remote interface's `getVoices()` only when `loadRemote` is true, and
  `_prepareReadAloud` (rj:84270-84271), on every popup open, passes
  `this._state.loggedIn`. That flag is `Zotero.Sync.Runner.enabled` at
  `_open()` (xr:270), refreshed on every `api-key` notification
  (xr:2879-2882); `enabled` is `_apiKey || Zotero.Sync.Data.Local.
  hasCredentials()` (`xpcom/sync/syncRunner.js` 36-40), and `setAPIKey`
  saves or removes the login before it notifies (`syncLocal.js` 135-157).
  The only other listener of `api-key` is Zotero's streamer (`streamer.js`
  41), which re-reads its key.
- The plugin's `loadVoices` hook (#121) passed Zotero's flag through, so
  the composite `getVoices` — Zotero's two tiers and every provider in one
  answer — never ran signed out. The 2026-09-15 notes had it as Still
  open, unverified; #110 had taken every user of plugin voices to be
  signed in.
- Zotero's first-run and Manage voices windows get their interface from
  the reader's own method with their own window (xr:1897-1935) and load
  the remote list without asking `loggedIn` (`useVoiceData`,
  `resource/reader/read-aloud-first-run.js` 3647, the reader bundle's copy
  at rj:40600), so they listed the plugin's voices signed out all along;
  only the player did not.
- Signed out, `TierSelect` (rj:38827-38875) pushes Local alone and, with
  one option, draws the "Log in to access Zotero Voices." row instead of
  the select, so #110's option rewrite never met an element.

The fix: the hook asks for the remote list whatever the flag on a reader
the hijack patched (`ownsInterface`, `diagnostics.liveVoiceList()` shows
`asked` beside `remote`); the composite skips Zotero's `getVoices` — and
does not build Zotero's interface, which opens its audio cache — while the
reader's own `_state.loggedIn` is false, and the credit calls answer none;
the dropdown wrapper hands `TierSelect`, known by its props (a boolean
`loggedIn`, a `tiers` Set, `onChange`, `onLogIn`; the popup's element has
no `tiers`), `loggedIn: true` while the manager lists any plugin voice
(`loginRowReplaced` in `providerTiers()`). The tab's flag rather than the
global one, because a live check can then set it on a fixture tab with
Zotero's own `setLoggedIn(false)` while the owner stays signed in:
Zotero's voices missing from that tab alone is the proof the skip ran.

The owner added one behavior: signed out, the Zotero section's Enable is
greyed, the reason on its line (`blocked` in `ui/provider-rows.ts`, a tier
already on keeps its Disable), and the pane follows a sign-in at once
through its own `api-key` observer. The reason,
`ztts-zotero-not-signed-in`, lost its `Edit →`: it now shows inside the
settings window on every platform, and macOS has no Edit → Settings.
`Zotero.Sync.Data.Local.hasCredentials` is an own property of that object,
so a live check can stand in for a sign-out by replacing it and firing the
notification (`cases/zotero-tiers.md` items 10-11).
