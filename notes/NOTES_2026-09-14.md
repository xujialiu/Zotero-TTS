## Zotero's block model cuts a paragraph mid-page, and its part-linking rule cannot rejoin a short last line (2026-09-14 15:33, issue #104)

**The user reported** that one sentence of their manuscript — `That said,
image magnification error correction is not required for longitudinal
monitoring of individuals [66] or for other instances in which no
significant change in AL is expected.`, page 13 of `2YW7BJTZ` — is read as
two, called it Zotero's, and asked for an issue and a way out. One research
run by `zotero-tester`, reads only on the owner's open tabs, settled it;
the evidence is on #104; the probes it ran are in this worktree's
`.tmp/zotero-dev/paragraph-parts/` (scratch, not committed), for the
verification brief once the fix is built.

**The cut is a chain boundary, not the splitter's.** Zotero 10.0.2-beta.9
(BuildID 20260909034856). Read Aloud's segments for a PDF come from the
structure pack: `sdt_segments_buildSDTReadAloudSegments` (reader.js:71256)
takes `collectChainTexts` (71329) and cuts each chain's text on its own in
`segmentChain` (71594), marking the first segment of every chain
`anchor = 'paragraphStart'` (71261–71264) — so a segment carrying that
mark in the middle of a sentence is the fingerprint of an unlinked block,
and the splitter never saw the sentence whole. A chain follows `nextPart`
and stops where there is none (`getPartChain`, 70938; the `break` at
70964). In this pack the sentence spans blocks 108 and 109, both
`paragraph`, body flow, neither with a part link; the pack has none at
all. The segments: 210 `That said, … not required for` (67 characters,
`anchor: null`) and 211 `longitudinal monitoring … is expected.` (111,
`paragraphStart`).

**Why Zotero's own rule cannot save it.** The block boundary is the
document worker's CRF clusterer's (worker.js:139946, `contClasses` 10–17
are "continues the previous block"): it predicted a block start on an
ordinary line, and there is no condition to cite. What could rejoin the
two is `markParagraphParts` (155838) → `canLinkParagraphs` (155896), whose
links `setPartLinks` (155593) writes, and it requires the first block's
last line to run full: `m1.lastLineRag > ragTolerance` → false (155928),
`RAG_THRESHOLD = 1` (155852; 1 % of the width, at least 1 pt, across a
page break), `lastLineRag = rawBlock.bbox[2] - lastChar.rect[2]` (155713).
Block 108's last line ends 36 pt short of the block's right edge;
`hasCompatibleWidth` (155878) fails too (418.68 against 423.96, tolerance
1). Every other test passes — same page, last character a lowercase
letter with no terminal punctuation (`plainContinuation`, 155915), next
block starting lowercase, indent 0.16 pt. The rule is written for a
paragraph continued across a page or a column, where the line before the
break is full; a block cut mid-paragraph ends on a short line by
definition, so the rule never fires for the case that needs it. The pack
was generated today with the current processor (`pdf: 14`,
`SDT_PROCESSOR_VERSIONS` at 156253): not a stale cache.

**How often.** Segments that end without terminal punctuation while the
next starts lowercase, all of them chain boundaries: 19 of 559 in the
manuscript (`Written informed` | `consent, …`, `Image analysis` | `was
performed …`, three of the first six across a page break), 23 of 560 in
its second copy `RW6PYBBQ`, 2 of 122 in the publisher's typeset version
`X7VC7KZS` (28 blocks, 1 part link, against 145 and 0). A Word document
printed to PDF is the shape that suffers; typeset PDFs rarely.

**Zotero drops linked citations itself.** `[66]` is not in segment 211:
`getElidedRanges` (reader.js:71417, "bracket/parenthesis groups that are
mostly linked text") with `isLinkGroup` (71445) elides it in
`compactChainText` (71490) at the end of `buildChainText`. So in a PDF
whose citations are link annotations, the plugin's bracket removal (#94,
#101) meets text that has no such brackets left; it matters for brackets
Zotero keeps — unlinked ones, and every EPUB and snapshot.

**Not the plugin's.** `diagnostics.skippedLines()` on the tab: `restored:
[]`, `excluded: 24` (page furniture) — #87's relink touched nothing here.
The bracket removal rewrites a segment's text after the cut, offsets
mapped back. Zotero's own voices get the same two halves.

**For the next brief.** `zotero_execute_js` has an eval window of about
8 s: a scan over three readers' segments in one call, or one reader's
scan plus its title and processor, came back as a bare `undefined`; one
reader per call, counts and scans apart, is safe. Zotero's own list is
`_internalReader._readAloudSegments.segments`; the structure is read from
`_internalReader._sdt.structure`, never `Zotero.SDT.getPack` (the #87
trap). The owner's paused player sat on segment 211, the second half of
this very sentence.

**What is next.** The fix is not designed; the plan goes on #104 once the
owner has chosen. The direction the evidence points to is #87's: the
`_loadSDT` shadow of `skipped-lines.ts` already walks the top-level
blocks once per tab before the sentences are cut, and a part link written
there between two consecutive body paragraphs — the first ending
mid-sentence, the second starting lowercase, directly below or the first
body block of the next page — would have Zotero's own chain walk, cut,
rects, highlights and follow do the rest, as they did for the restored
line. Merging the two segments after the cut would mean rebuilding
positions, rects and timestamps by hand against Zotero's own mapper.

## The cut paragraph joined back before the sentences are cut, and the runner's first kit (2026-09-14 16:52, issue #104)

**The fix** is `src/read-aloud/paragraph-parts.ts`, the pure sieve and the
join, run by `skipped-lines.ts` in its `_loadSDT` shadow after the #87
restore on the same waived structure — one walk, one record
(`restored` and `joined`), one diagnostic (`skippedLines()` gained
`joinEnabled` and `joined`). Four tests, all of which a pair must pass:
A and B consecutive top-level leaf `paragraph` blocks, neither excluded,
only excluded blocks between them (a page number), A without `nextPart`
and B without `previousPart`; A ending mid-sentence (`endsMidSentence`);
B starting with a lowercase letter, a citation bracket, or a short bracket
group and then a lowercase word; B where A's next line would be — below A
within `MAX_GAP_PT` 36 with the columns overlapping, at the top of the
next column, or on the next page. The join is the part link the worker
refused, `A.nextPart = [B]` / `B.previousPart = [A]` through `cloneInto`;
Zotero's `getPartChain` then follows it, `segmentChain` cuts the whole
sentence, `_materializeSourcePositions` gives it its rects — three for
this sentence: the tail of the line at y 249, the whole line at 225, the
head of the line at 202 — and the `paragraphStart` mark moves to the real
paragraph. The switch `readAloud.joinSplitSentences`, default on, a pane
row under the #87 one; 13 unit tests on blocks 108–110 as measured, 2 on
the wiring.

**Verified live twice**, on fresh standalone copies of the three PDFs (the
owner's tabs had loaded their structures before the build; a copy gets a
fresh pack). 1.12.8-beta: 17 / 21 / 2 joins on `2YW7BJTZ` / `RW6PYBBQ` /
`X7VC7KZS`, the mid-sentence chain boundaries 19 → 2, 23 → 2, 2 → 0; one
of the two left a real split, `…the five selected features` | `(Table 4)
exhibited …`, refused because the bracket rule admitted only a digit.
1.12.8-beta2 widened it to a bracket group before a lowercase word: 18 /
22 / 2 joins, 1 / 1 / 0 left, the one a run-in heading before a figure
(`Image analysis` | `was performed …`, blocks 49 → 53 with a paragraph
and an `auxiliary` image between) that the sieve rightly refuses; the
segment count and the `paragraphStart` count each down by exactly the
join count (559 → 541, 195 → 177; 560 → 538, 197 → 175; 122 → 120,
36 → 34). The switch off: 210 and 211 back. No plugin error either run.

**What the runs taught.** Zotero unloads reader tabs beyond
`MAX_LOADED_TABS = 5` by `timeUnselected` (`Zotero_Tabs.unloadUnusedTabs`):
the first run's copies pushed the owner's three manuscript tabs out, and
with them their paused players — the tabs and positions survive, the
players do not; a kit holds at most one copy open at a time now
(`03-open-fresh-copy.js` erases the previous one first). The debug line
cuts each side to 30 characters; pages in it are 1-based where
`joined.page` and `pageIndex` are 0-based. **The runner's first kit**
(`test/zotero-dev/scripts/paragraph-parts/`, twelve scripts, written by
the tester during the beta run, run unchanged on beta2): the beta run cost
213k tokens, 68 calls and 20 minutes with the kit still to write; the
beta2 re-run 131k tokens, 40 calls and 9 minutes — against the 2026-09-13
angle-brackets verification's 58M context tokens, 210 calls and 73
minutes. The scripts never entered the tester's context; the README did.
