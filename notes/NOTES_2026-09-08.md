## Zotero NFC-normalizes an EPUB's text, and the highlight patch's === check goes dark on a document stored decomposed (2026-09-08 00:52, issue #74)

**The follow-up to issue #73.** The reporter answered that Alina and
Emil highlight by sentence too, and that the sentence highlight misses
the start or the end of what is read, in EPUB and PDF. A second bridge
run, on Romanian text with ă/ș/ț in three encodings — comma-below
precomposed, cedilla precomposed, NFD — as an EPUB zipped by Python and
a PDF printed by Chrome headless (`--headless=new
--no-pdf-header-footer --print-to-pdf`), Azure-Alina, highlight at word:

- Every segment of both files got N word timestamps = its word count;
  no word was dropped in any encoding. Azure echoes back the exact form
  it was sent (the spans hold `foloseşte`, `ţăranii` verbatim on the
  cedilla paragraph, the decomposed forms on the PDF), so
  `alignWordsToText`'s exact search always matches. The diacritics
  hypothesis is dead.
- The sentence highlight covered the spoken segment exactly in all 26
  segments: in the EPUB head + word + tail reconstruct the segment; in
  the PDF the sentence rects equal `activeSegment.sourcePosition.rects`,
  multi-line sentences included, and with a wordless voice's stand-in
  too. The reporter's "beginning or end not highlighted" did not
  reproduce. Untested ground: inline elements splitting a paragraph's
  text nodes (issues #3/#4), soft hyphens and NBSP, a sentence across
  two blocks. It needs the reporter's file.
- Zotero's highlight default is `sentence` (`defaults/preferences/
  zotero.js` 1814). The reporter almost certainly never changed it,
  which is the whole of "every voice highlights the sentence".

**What Zotero does with text forms.** An EPUB's text reaches Read Aloud
NFC-normalized: reader.js 62066–62074 (DeltaMap, "position shifts
between NFC-normalized text and the original DOM text … origPos =
nfcPos − getDelta(deltaMap, nfcPos)") and the SDT text-node comment at
63023 ff.; `addSegment` (71223) only collapses whitespace
(`normalizeText`, 71709). `toDisplayedRange()` returns the document's
own form. PDF.js hands ș/ț decomposed (`U+0073 U+0326`) and î
recomposed (`U+00EE`), and the PDF path has no string comparison at
all. Zotero's word CFI on the NFD paragraph was right
(`epubcfi(/6/2!/4/8/1,:96,:107)`, 11 original characters for the
9-character NFC word).

**The bug (issue #74).** highlight-style.ts verifies the word position
by `displayedText(view, selector) === expected` (597) and the sentence
by `normText(segment.text)` against the displayed text (553/568);
`normText` (473) and `displayedText` (475) collapse whitespace only, no
Unicode normalization. On an NFD paragraph both fail: `hiddenPrimary`
is set (609), `_getSpotlightColor` answers `#00000000` for the segment
key (768), and `preparePiecesDOM` deletes both piece slots — nothing at
all is drawn. Then `repairWordPosition` returns at 591 when the
granularity is not word without resetting the flag; only the
`!state.popupOpen` branch (588) clears it, so the transparent primary
survives a switch to Sentence until the player is reopened. `logChange`
(266–271) dedupes per key for the instance's whole life, so the
`painting the primary transparent` line can be missing from a later
debug capture; `#00000000` is the proof either way. Severity minor: few
EPUBs are stored NFD.

**Beside the point.** Switching a playing PDF to Azure-Ioana MAI-Voice-2
got `Azure socket closed before turn.end` five times in a row
(00:33–00:34), manager and controller at `_error: "network"`, stuck on
the segment until a skip and a resume; Alina never dropped one in some
40 segments. Reported correctly, no hang, no retry of the plugin's own.
The Read Aloud AudioContext again read `suspended` at `currentTime` 0
throughout while the positions advanced, as on 2026-09-07.

**Fixtures.** The generator and the files went into
`test/fixtures/ro-diacritics/` untracked (`build.py`, `paragraphs.txt`,
`ro-diacritics.epub`, `ro-diacritics.pdf`, `ro-diacritics.html`): three
paragraphs × four sentences, one paragraph per encoding. The fix commits
them or not.

**Checklist items the tester drafted, for the fix** (test/zotero-dev.md,
the highlight section):

- **A document stored in NFD keeps its highlight.** An EPUB whose text
  nodes hold decomposed diacritics (`s U+0326` for ș, `a U+0306` for ă)
  read at Word with a voice that has word timestamps. Check: on a segment
  inside that paragraph, `Zotero.ZoteroTTS.diagnostics.highlight()` and
  `view._getSpotlightColor('ReadAloudActiveSegment')` through the primary
  view. Expected: the color is the word color (`#3478f6b3` at the default
  alpha), not `#00000000`; `sentenceSlot` is `ours` and `sentencePieces`
  holds a head and a tail. Fixture: `ro-diacritics.epub`, paragraph 3.
  Was, before the fix: nothing drawn at all while that paragraph is read.
- **The transparent primary is cleared when Word is left.** After the
  NFD case above, set `reader.readAloud.highlightGranularity` to
  `sentence` without closing the player. Check:
  `view._getSpotlightColor('ReadAloudActiveSegment')`. Expected: the
  sentence color (`#ffff00b3` at the default). Was: `#00000000`.
- **Azure word timestamps survive every Romanian encoding.** The same
  fixture, three paragraphs. Check: the `[zotero-tts] azure: N word
  timestamps for M chars` line per segment against the segment's word
  count. Expected: N equals the word count for all twelve body segments —
  8/13/9/12 per paragraph in the EPUB and in the PDF (the PDF's char
  counts are larger: PDF.js returns ș/ț decomposed); no `no word
  timestamps` line for Alina.
