The clean handoff used fixture A segment 7 (132 characters): `A reader that skips by paragraph should land here, at the beginning of the second paragraph, and then at the beginning of the third.` The old `local::af_jadzia` buffer duration was `6.696` seconds and the target `local::af_jessica` buffer duration was `6.432` seconds. Their complete timestamp arrays are in [evidence.json](evidence.json).

The failure is a leading timestamp issue, rather than a later ordering issue. The old array starts at `-0.004541666666666666` and ends its first word at `0.07045833333333329`; every later start is monotonic. The target starts at `0.02537500000000001` and ends its first word at `0.10037500000000002`; its later starts are also monotonic. Thus the negative start is about `0.075` seconds before the first word end, and there is no later nonmonotonic start. The numeric probe showed the same pattern: first starts `-0.0032916666666666927` and `-0.007916666666666627`, followed by monotonic rows.

The clean diagnostic sequence was `preparing` → `sentence` with `prepared: [7,8]`, then `committed` at sentence index 8, offset 0, after the old source naturally ended. The diagnostic recorded old audio alive at target readiness and no numeric word stop. This rules out target latency as the cause: the earlier uncached attempt also reached target readiness at 747 ms with the old segment still at index 7, and made the same `invalid-timing-order` decision. The matcher currently rejects any timestamp with `start < 0` in `src/core/voice-switch.ts:30-32`, so `src/core/voice-switch.ts:66-67` returns `invalid-timing-order` before it considers later shared boundaries.

The actual scripts are under [scripts/voice-switch-kokoro](../../scripts/voice-switch-kokoro/README.md): baseline and mute/sync setup, fixture open, trusted clock probe, clean real handoff, exploratory reverse probe, real numeric alignment, and cleanup. The earlier reverse probe wrapped a reader-realm promise and left two test-only `Permission denied ... then` console entries; it is excluded from the clean result. No production source was changed during this run.
